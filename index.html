Before you measure
Preparation
  • smoke_free
    No smoking, coffee, food or exercise for 30 minutes beforehand
  • volume_off
    Quiet surroundings
  • thermostat
    Comfortable room temperature
  • timer
    Rest for 5 minutes before measuring
  • voice_over_off
    Don't talk during or between measurements
Position
  • chair
    Sit with your back supported and both feet flat on the floor
  • front_hand
    Bare arm resting on a table, the middle of the upper arm at heart level
  • straighten
    Use a cuff that fits your arm circumference (small, normal or large)
  • verified
    Use a validated electronic upper-arm monitor

Install HeartPass
  1. ios_share
    Tap the Share button in the toolbar. In Safari it may be hidden behind the ••• button.
  2. add_box
    Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. check_circle
    Leave Open as Web App switched on and tap Add.

The installed app keeps its own data, separate from the browser. To take existing measurements along, export them in the settings first and import them in the installed app.

  1. more_vert
    Open the browser menu (⋮).
  2. add_to_home_screen
    Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. check_circle
    Confirm with Install.
  • install_desktop
    Chrome / Edge: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or choose Install HeartPass in the browser menu.
  • laptop_mac
    Safari (Mac): choose File → Add to Dock.
